### ⚠️ Avoiding Fake Cartridges

**Signs of a reproduction/fake:**
- Price too good to be true (Pokémon Emerald for $15 = fake)
- Seller has many copies of rare games
- Label looks "off" - wrong colors, blurry
- No battery save (for games that should have it)
- "Repro" or "reproduction" in listing (at least they're honest)

**Tips:**
- Ask for photos of the circuit board
- Buy from sellers with good feedback
- GBA Pokémon games are most commonly faked
- When in doubt, pay a bit more for guaranteed authentic

### 🔧 Dumping Your Cartridges

Once you own cartridges, dump them legally with:

| Device | Systems | Price | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|-------|
| **Epilogue GB Operator** | GB/GBC/GBA | ~$50 | Best for Game Boy family |
| **Retrode 2** | NES/SNES/Genesis/N64/GB | ~$85 | Multi-system, excellent |
| **INLretro Dumper** | NES/SNES/GB | ~$60 | Open source |
| **Sanni Cart Reader** | Many systems | ~$50 (DIY) | Requires assembly |

**Simple workflow:**
1. Buy cartridge
2. Insert into dumper → USB to computer
3. Use included software to create ROM file
4. Copy ROM to `gaming-server/roms/[system]/`
5. Done! Legal backup for camp use
