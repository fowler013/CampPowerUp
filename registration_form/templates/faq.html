🎮 What is Camp Power-Up?
Camp Power-Up is a Nintendo Switch gaming camp where kids come together to play multiplayer games in a fun, social environment. We focus on cooperative and competitive multiplayer experiences that help kids make friends, develop teamwork skills, and have a blast gaming together. We offer both 5-day and 2-day camp sessions throughout the year.
👧 What ages can attend?
Camp Power-Up is open to children in Kindergarten through 8th grade. We have group activities appropriately to ensure all campers have fun with peers at their level.

🕹️ Does my child need to bring a Nintendo Switch?
Nope! We have multiple TVs and Nintendo Switch consoles already set up for group play. We also have extra Switch consoles available for handheld play. Your child is welcome to bring their own Switch if they prefer, but it's absolutely not required.
🍎 Do you provide lunch?
No, lunch is not provided. Please make sure your camper brings:
  • Their own packed lunch
  • Snacks for throughout the day
  • A water bottle

We take a break each day to enjoy lunch and spend about an hour at Patriot Park across the street for some outdoor playtime!

📋 What are the camp rules?

  • LABEL EVERYTHING! - Please put your child's name on all belongings
  • Be a good winner - Celebrate your victories graciously
  • Be a good loser - Accept defeat with a positive attitude
  • Game ratings matter - No overly violent, scary, or mature (M-rated) games

✨ Anything else I should know?

  • Prizes & Trophies: We sometimes have prizes and trophies for tournaments and special achievements!

  • Stay Connected: Join our Facebook group to stay updated on camp news and connect with other camp families
  • Payment Confirmation: We will confirm your registration through whichever payment app you use (Venmo, PayPal, etc.)
