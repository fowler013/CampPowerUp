Set up access before camp day

Discord is optional. Parents do not need a Discord account for their child to attend camp or be picked up. Staff handle check-in and pickup in person for families who do not use Discord.

  1. Verify the parent. Use the trusted registration/contact process, then separately verify their exact Discord account.
  2. Link Discord if the family chooses. A verified link grants access only to that parent's camper, not permission to pick up. No link is a normal, valid choice.
  3. Authorize pickups separately. Get parent confirmation for each person, including the linked parent. Emergency contacts are not automatically authorized.

This page does not record physical attendance or show whether Kit is enabled. CampPowerUp holds the records; Kit is a private interface. Use Camp-day check-in & pickup to confirm physical arrival and release for every camper.

Helping a parent who is new to Discord

Offer help only if the family wants to use Discord. Let the parent create and control their own account; never request their password. Guide them through using the private interface, then manually verify the parent and exact account before linking. If they prefer not to sign up, continue with in-person staff check-in and pickup.

What goes in a verification reference?

Enter a nonsecret reference to the private verification record, such as parent-check-014 or pickup-ok-014. Use letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores (up to 128 characters). Do not enter contact details, conversations or secrets. Typing a reference does not verify identity: complete the manual checks first.
